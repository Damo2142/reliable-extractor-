CHW-PLANT I/O Terminal Map (MPS)
=================================

INPUTS (AI/BI):
  IN1   CHW-FLOW       AI   CHW System Flow (GPM)           [core]
  IN2   CHWS-T         AI   CHW Supply Temperature          [core]
  IN3   OAT            AI   Outside Air Temperature         [core]
  IN4   OAH            AI   Outside Air Humidity            [core]
  IN5   CHW-FLOW       AI   CHW System Flow                 [core]
  IN6   CHLR1-ALARM    BI   Chiller 1 Alarm Contact         [chiller]
  IN7   CHLR2-ALARM    BI   Chiller 2 Alarm Contact         [chiller]
  IN8   CHLR1-CHWS-T   AI   Chiller 1 Leaving CHW Temp     [chiller]
  IN9   CHLR1-CHWR-T   AI   Chiller 1 Entering CHW Temp    [chiller]
  IN10  CHLR2-CHWS-T   AI   Chiller 2 Leaving CHW Temp     [chiller]
  IN11  CHLR2-CHWR-T   AI   Chiller 2 Entering CHW Temp    [chiller]
  IN16  PCHWP1-STS     BI   Primary CHW Pump 1 Status       [pri-pump]
  IN17  PCHWP2-STS     BI   Primary CHW Pump 2 Status       [pri-pump]
  IN20  SCHWP1-STS     BI   Secondary CHW Pump 1 Status     [sec-pump]
  IN21  SCHWP2-STS     BI   Secondary CHW Pump 2 Status     [sec-pump]
  IN24  CHW-PRESS1     AI   CHW DP Sensor 1                 [sec-pump]
  IN25  CHW-PRESS2     AI   CHW DP Sensor 2                 [sec-pump]
  IN26  CDWP1-STS      BI   CW Pump 1 Status                [cdwp]
  IN27  CDWP2-STS      BI   CW Pump 2 Status                [cdwp]
  IN28  CT1-STS        BI   Cooling Tower 1 Fan Status      [tower]
  IN29  CT2-STS        BI   Cooling Tower 2 Fan Status      [tower]
  IN30  CDW-ENT-T      AI   Condenser Water Entering Temp   [tower]
  IN31  CDW-LVG-T      AI   Condenser Water Leaving Temp    [tower]
  IN32  CHW-BYP-T      AI   CHW Bypass Temperature          [bypass-valve]
  IN33  CHLR1-CHW-ISO  BI   Chiller 1 CHW Iso Valve ES      [iso-valves]
  IN34  CHLR2-CHW-ISO  BI   Chiller 2 CHW Iso Valve ES      [iso-valves]
  IN35  CHLR1-CDW-ISO  BI   Chiller 1 CDW Iso Valve ES      [iso-valves, tower]
  IN36  CHLR2-CDW-ISO  BI   Chiller 2 CDW Iso Valve ES      [iso-valves, tower]
  IN37  MAKEUP-WTR     AI   Makeup Water Flow               [makeup-water]

OUTPUTS (AO/BO):
  OUT1  CHLR1-ENABLE   BO   Chiller 1 Enable Contact        [chiller]
  OUT2  CHLR2-ENABLE   BO   Chiller 2 Enable Contact        [chiller]
  OUT5  PCHWP1-S/S     BO   Primary CHW Pump 1 S/S          [pri-pump]
  OUT6  PCHWP2-S/S     BO   Primary CHW Pump 2 S/S          [pri-pump]
  OUT9  SCHWP1-S/S     BO   Secondary CHW Pump 1 S/S        [sec-pump]
  OUT10 SCHWP2-S/S     BO   Secondary CHW Pump 2 S/S        [sec-pump]
  OUT13 SCHWP1-SPEED   AO   Secondary CHW Pump 1 VFD Speed  [sec-pump]
  OUT14 SCHWP2-SPEED   AO   Secondary CHW Pump 2 VFD Speed  [sec-pump]
  OUT17 CDWP1-S/S      BO   CW Pump 1 S/S                   [cdwp]
  OUT18 CDWP2-S/S      BO   CW Pump 2 S/S                   [cdwp]
  OUT21 CT1-S/S        BO   Cooling Tower 1 Fan S/S         [tower]
  OUT22 CT2-S/S        BO   Cooling Tower 2 Fan S/S         [tower]
  OUT23 CT1-SPD        AO   Cooling Tower 1 Fan VFD Speed   [tower]
  OUT24 CT2-SPD        AO   Cooling Tower 2 Fan VFD Speed   [tower]
  OUT25 CT-BYP-VLV     AO   Tower CW Bypass Valve           [tower-bypass]
  OUT26 CHW-BYP-VLV    AO   CHW Bypass Valve                [bypass-valve]
  OUT27 CHLR1-CHW-ISO  BO   Chiller 1 CHW Isolation Valve   [iso-valves]
  OUT28 CHLR2-CHW-ISO  BO   Chiller 2 CHW Isolation Valve   [iso-valves]
  OUT29 CHLR1-CDW-ISO  BO   Chiller 1 CDW Isolation Valve   [iso-valves, tower]
  OUT30 CHLR2-CDW-ISO  BO   Chiller 2 CDW Isolation Valve   [iso-valves, tower]
