CHW-PLANT I/O Terminal Map (MPS) — Packed Layout
==================================================

Design goal: minimize expansion modules.
  MPS base: 12 IN, 8 OUT
  Each MPP-IO-U expansion: +12 IN, +8 OUT
  Standard air cooled (2ch, 2+2): 17 IN, 8 OUT → base only
  Standard tower (2ch, 2+2+2+2): 23 IN, 14 OUT → 1 expansion
  Full tower + iso + bypass: 29 IN, 20 OUT → 2 expansions

INPUTS (packed, no gaps):
  IN1   CHWS-T         AI   CHW Supply Temperature          [core]
  IN2   CHWR-T         AI   CHW Return Temperature          [core]
  IN3   OAT            AI   Outside Air Temperature         [core]
  IN4   OAH            AI   Outside Air Humidity            [core]
  IN5   CHW-FLOW       AI   CHW System Flow                 [core]
  IN6   CHLR1-ALARM    BI   Chiller 1 Alarm Contact         [chiller]
  IN7   CHLR2-ALARM    BI   Chiller 2 Alarm Contact         [chiller]
  IN8   CHLR1-CHWS-T   AI   Chiller 1 Leaving CHW Temp     [chiller]
  IN9   CHLR1-CHWR-T   AI   Chiller 1 Entering CHW Temp    [chiller]
  IN10  CHLR2-CHWS-T   AI   Chiller 2 Leaving CHW Temp     [chiller]
  IN11  CHLR2-CHWR-T   AI   Chiller 2 Entering CHW Temp    [chiller]
  IN12  PCHWP1-STS     BI   Primary CHW Pump 1 Status       [pri-pump]
  --- expansion 1 starts at IN13 ---
  IN13  PCHWP2-STS     BI   Primary CHW Pump 2 Status       [pri-pump]
  IN14  SCHWP1-STS     BI   Secondary CHW Pump 1 Status     [sec-pump]
  IN15  SCHWP2-STS     BI   Secondary CHW Pump 2 Status     [sec-pump]
  IN16  CHW-PRESS1     AI   CHW DP Sensor 1                 [sec-pump]
  IN17  CHW-PRESS2     AI   CHW DP Sensor 2                 [sec-pump]
  IN18  CDWP1-STS      BI   CW Pump 1 Status                [cdwp]
  IN19  CDWP2-STS      BI   CW Pump 2 Status                [cdwp]
  IN20  CT1-FAN-STS    BI   Cooling Tower 1 Fan Status      [tower]
  IN21  CT2-FAN-STS    BI   Cooling Tower 2 Fan Status      [tower]
  IN22  CDW-ENT-T      AI   Condenser Water Entering Temp   [tower]
  IN23  CDW-LVG-T      AI   Condenser Water Leaving Temp    [tower]
  IN24  CHW-BYP-T      AI   CHW Bypass Temperature          [bypass-valve]
  --- expansion 2 starts at IN25 ---
  IN25  CHLR1-CHW-ISO  BI   Chiller 1 CHW Iso Valve ES      [iso-valves]
  IN26  CHLR2-CHW-ISO  BI   Chiller 2 CHW Iso Valve ES      [iso-valves]
  IN27  CHLR1-CDW-ISO  BI   Chiller 1 CDW Iso Valve ES      [iso-valves, tower]
  IN28  CHLR2-CDW-ISO  BI   Chiller 2 CDW Iso Valve ES      [iso-valves, tower]
  IN29  MAKEUP-WTR     AI   Makeup Water Flow               [makeup-water]

OUTPUTS (packed, no gaps):
  OUT1  CHLR1-S/S       BO   Chiller 1 Enable               [chiller]
  OUT2  CHLR2-S/S       BO   Chiller 2 Enable               [chiller]
  OUT3  PCHWP1-S/S      BO   Primary CHW Pump 1 S/S         [pri-pump]
  OUT4  PCHWP2-S/S      BO   Primary CHW Pump 2 S/S         [pri-pump]
  OUT5  SCHWP1-S/S      BO   Secondary CHW Pump 1 S/S       [sec-pump]
  OUT6  SCHWP2-S/S      BO   Secondary CHW Pump 2 S/S       [sec-pump]
  OUT7  SCHWP1-SPEED    AO   Secondary CHW Pump 1 VFD Speed [sec-pump]
  OUT8  SCHWP2-SPEED    AO   Secondary CHW Pump 2 VFD Speed [sec-pump]
  --- expansion 1 starts at OUT9 ---
  OUT9  CDWP1-S/S       BO   CW Pump 1 S/S                  [cdwp]
  OUT10 CDWP2-S/S       BO   CW Pump 2 S/S                  [cdwp]
  OUT11 CT1-FAN-S/S     BO   Cooling Tower 1 Fan S/S        [tower]
  OUT12 CT2-FAN-S/S     BO   Cooling Tower 2 Fan S/S        [tower]
  OUT13 CT1-FAN-SPEED   AO   Cooling Tower 1 Fan VFD Speed  [tower]
  OUT14 CT2-FAN-SPEED   AO   Cooling Tower 2 Fan VFD Speed  [tower]
  OUT15 CT-BYP-VLV      AO   Tower CW Bypass Valve          [tower-bypass]
  OUT16 CHW-BYP-VLV     AO   CHW Bypass Valve               [bypass-valve]
  --- expansion 2 starts at OUT17 ---
  OUT17 CHLR1-CHW-ISO   BO   Chiller 1 CHW Isolation Valve  [iso-valves]
  OUT18 CHLR2-CHW-ISO   BO   Chiller 2 CHW Isolation Valve  [iso-valves]
  OUT19 CHLR1-CDW-ISO   BO   Chiller 1 CDW Isolation Valve  [iso-valves, tower]
  OUT20 CHLR2-CDW-ISO   BO   Chiller 2 CDW Isolation Valve  [iso-valves, tower]

EXPANSION SUMMARY:
  Air cooled (no tower):         12 IN, 8 OUT  → MPS base only
  Air cooled + bypass:           13 IN, 9 OUT  → 1 expansion
  Tower (no iso):                23 IN, 15 OUT → 1 expansion
  Tower + bypass:                24 IN, 16 OUT → 1 expansion
  Tower + iso + bypass:          29 IN, 20 OUT → 2 expansions
  Tower + iso + bypass + makeup: 29 IN, 20 OUT → 2 expansions
