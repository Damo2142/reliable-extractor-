      Byte 3 = checksum/validation byte (varies per program)
      Byte 4 = repeat of byte 1 (high byte of size)
    
    BYTECODE STREAM FORMAT:
      Same as our cbas_compiler.py output:
      01 [line_num LE16] — line marker
      1A [len] [ASCII text] — REM comment
      [other opcodes for IF, THEN, GOTO, assignments, etc.]
      
      REM comments are preserved inline in bytecode as:
        0x1A [length_byte] [ASCII_text]
      This matches our cbas_compiler exactly.
